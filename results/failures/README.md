# Validation failure examples

These four examples use the original YOLO11n seed-42 checkpoint. Green boxes are matched detections; amber boxes are ground truth missed at confidence 0.25 and IoU 0.5; red boxes are false positives; yellow boxes are matching low-confidence predictions. The overlays are generated by `python scripts/failure_gallery.py` from the saved validation predictions. [Measurements](measurements.json) give the exact confidence and overlap values.

| Image | What happened | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| [PennPed00015](PennPed00015.png) | A high-confidence box covers overlapping people but has IoU 0.462 with the remaining ground-truth person, so it counts as an FP and the person as an FN. A low-confidence candidate overlaps that person at IoU 0.777. | FP score 0.932; candidate score 0.033. |
| [FudanPed00025](FudanPed00025.png) | A person partly hidden in a crowd is localized but rejected by the 0.25 operating threshold. | Best low-score box: IoU 0.908, score 0.021. |
| [PennPed00044](PennPed00044.png) | The distant, small pedestrian is localized but rejected by the threshold. | Best low-score box: IoU 0.888, score 0.152. |
| [FudanPed00016](FudanPed00016.png) | Two boxes are false positives under the supplied annotations. Visual inspection shows additional people near those boxes, so incomplete annotation is a plausible cause; this cannot be established from the masks alone. | FP scores 0.584 and 0.323. |

These are **validation** examples, selected before any test-set evaluation. The labels describe the evaluator's decisions, not a claim that every unlabeled visible person is a real annotation error.
